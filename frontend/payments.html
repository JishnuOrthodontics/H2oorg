Razorpay Payments

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Secure digital payments for HARIESH AND HELDIN ORGANIZATION (H2O)

H2O uses Razorpay, a RBI-authorised payment aggregator, for card, UPI, and other digital methods enabled on our merchant account. We do not store your full card number or UPI PIN on our servers.

1. Merchant

Payments are collected by HARIESH AND HELDIN ORGANIZATION (H2O), Chendamangalam, Ernakulam, Kerala. Support: h2organization2025@gmail.com · +91 79073 49501.

2. How checkout works

  • At the store POS, staff may offer Cash, Card/UPI via Razorpay, or a split (part cash + part Razorpay).
  • For Razorpay, our system creates a payment order; you complete payment in Razorpay’s secure Checkout.
  • On success, payment is verified by cryptographic signature and/or Razorpay webhooks before the sale is marked paid and stock/loyalty are updated.

3. Security

  • Card and UPI credentials are entered only on Razorpay-controlled pages/SDKs.
  • Our API stores order and payment reference IDs needed for reconciliation and refunds — not CVV or full PAN.
  • Website and API traffic use HTTPS.

4. Failed, cancelled, or duplicate payments

  • If you cancel Checkout or a payment fails, no sale is completed for that attempt.
  • If amount is debited but the bill is not generated (network drop), contact us with the UPI/bank reference or Razorpay payment ID. We will reconcile against Razorpay and refund or complete the invoice as appropriate.
  • Refunds, where due, are initiated to the original payment method per Razorpay and bank timelines.

5. Fees & settlements

Razorpay charges merchant fees under our commercial agreement with them. Customers pay the invoice total shown (GST-inclusive pricing as displayed). Settlements to our bank account follow Razorpay’s settlement cycle.
