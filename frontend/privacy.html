2. What we collect

  • Customer profile: name, phone, email, loyalty points, scent preferences, birthday/anniversary (if shared), and remarks you provide.
  • Transaction data: invoices, items purchased, GST amounts, payment method, and related references.
  • Payment metadata: when you pay digitally, Razorpay and banks process card/UPI details. We receive confirmation references (e.g. order/payment IDs), not full card numbers.
  • Staff accounts: usernames, roles, and shift/audit logs for POS security.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information and logs needed to run and secure the website and API.

3. Why we use your data

  • Complete sales, issue invoices, and provide after-sales support.
  • Loyalty, CRM, and optional occasion reminders (SMS/WhatsApp where configured and permitted).
  • GST, accounting, and legal record-keeping.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and improving store operations.

4. Legal bases / consent

We process data that is necessary to fulfil a purchase or store visit, comply with tax/law, or protect our systems. Marketing messages (e.g. birthday offers) are sent where you have an existing relationship and/or have not opted out, and in line with telecom/DLT and messaging-provider rules. You may ask us to stop promotional messages at any time.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Razorpay — payment processing (Razorpay Privacy Policy).
  • Hosting & infrastructure — e.g. Cloudflare (website), Render (API), Supabase (database), under their security controls.
  • Messaging providers (e.g. MSG91 / Twilio) — only phone/message content needed to deliver SMS or WhatsApp you are due to receive.
  • Authorities — when required by law, GST authorities, or valid legal process.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, access-controlled staff logins, encrypted storage of sensitive customer contact fields where implemented, and least-privilege practices for the POS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use strong PINs for staff accounts and report suspected misuse promptly.

7. Retention

Transaction and tax records are kept for periods required under GST and Income Tax laws (generally several years). CRM profiles are retained while you remain a customer or until you request deletion, subject to legal retention needs.

8. Your rights

Subject to law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent for marketing. Contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity and keep data required for invoices or disputes.

9. Children

Our products and site are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly create CRM profiles for children without a parent/guardian.

10. Cookies & local storage

The Staff POS may store a login token in your browser’s local storage for session continuity. Public browsing uses only what is needed for the site to function.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top will be revised when material changes are made.

12. Contact / grievance

For privacy requests or complaints, email h2organization2025@gmail.com or call +91 79073 49501. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month where feasible.
