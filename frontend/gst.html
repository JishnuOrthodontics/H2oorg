GST Compliance

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · HARIESH AND HELDIN ORGANIZATION (H2O), Ernakulam, Kerala

H2O is a perfume / fragrance retailer operating from Kerala. Our POS calculates and displays GST on taxable supplies. This page explains how we treat tax on invoices. It is general information, not tax advice. Official GSTIN will appear on tax invoices once registered/updated with the authorities.

2. How GST appears on bills

  • For typical intra-state (Kerala) retail sales, our POS shows a taxable value with CGST 9% + SGST 9% (18% combined) where that rate applies to the product category, unless a different notified rate applies.
  • Catalog and cart totals are generally shown GST-inclusive; the invoice breaks out taxable value and tax components.
  • Inter-state supplies, if any, would use IGST as required by law instead of CGST+SGST.

3. Own website vs marketplace

We sell our own inventory through our boutique and website tools. We are not acting as a GST “e-commerce operator” collecting TCS under Section 52 for other sellers. GST on our own supplies is discharged under the CGST/SGST/IGST Acts as applicable to a normal registered (or otherwise compliant) supplier.

4. Invoices & records

  • A bill / tax invoice is issued for taxable supplies as required.
  • Please retain your invoice for returns, warranty queries, or input tax credit (if you are a registered business entitled to ITC).
  • We retain transaction records for the periods prescribed under GST and income-tax law.

5. Input tax credit (B2B)

If you are a GST-registered business purchasing for furtherance of business, ensure your GSTIN is shared at billing so a proper tax invoice can be issued. ITC eligibility depends on your compliance and the GST law; we cannot guarantee ITC for every buyer.

6. Corrections

If an invoice has a clerical error, contact us promptly with the invoice number. Credit/debit notes or revised documents will be issued where the GST law requires.
